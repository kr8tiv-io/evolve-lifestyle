Cypress Hills: three huts above Elkwater In winter the Cypress Hills are lodgepole, a low sun, and prairie snow. Medicine Lodge , Tom Trott Hut , and Spruce Coulee are Alberta Parks huts near Elkwater, on the Alberta side of the provincial park. The Saskatchewan half of these hills is a different place. Medicine Lodge and Tom Trott sit about 6 km south of Elkwater. Winter and spring are skis or snowshoes only, from Spring Creek Shelter, about 5 km on Murray Hill Road, or from the Visitor Centre, about 6 km on the Horseshoe Canyon trail. Spring Creek is 10 km of groomed classic and skate. Spruce Coulee is about 14 km east on the reservoir: about 200 m in summer, about 4.5 km from Reesor Viewpoint in winter, so bring a sled. Each hut sleeps six at $100 CAD a night, with a wood stove and no drinking water. Medicine Lodge is one dark room with cell reception. Tom Trott has two bedrooms, solar light, no outlets, and a signal. Spruce Coulee has two bedrooms, no lights, and no cell reception. Firewood and dishes run November 1 to April 30. Bring a stove and fuel. In at 4 p.m., out at noon, no pets except service dogs. On 27 November 2026 the calendar ran through May 26, 2027. Book 180 days ahead at 1-877-537-2757 or Shop.AlbertaParks.ca, and cancel at least 72 hours out. Visitor Centre: 403-893-3833, extension 5. Shadow Lake Lodge Shadow Lake Lodge is private cabins, a custodian, and hot showers at the end of a trail that has already crossed avalanche paths. From Redearth Creek, 19.6 km west of Banff on the Trans-Canada, allow 14 km, 440 m, and 4 to 5 hours on skis, snowshoes, or a fat bike.
