The Powder Highway BC is a winter loop through the Kootenay Rockies in southeastern British Columbia. Kootenay Rockies Tourism defines it as eight alpine resorts: Revelstoke Mountain Resort, Kicking Horse Mountain Resort in Golden, Fernie Alpine Resort, Whitewater Ski Resort above Nelson, RED Mountain Resort in Rossland, Panorama Mountain Resort above Invermere, Kimberley Alpine Resort, and the ski hill at Fairmont Hot Springs Resort.
