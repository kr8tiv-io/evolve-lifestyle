# EVOLVE — Lifestyle Brand Brief

**Version 1.0 · Framework build · June 2026**
*The lifestyle / apparel arm of Evolve Eco Blasting. Same name, same logo, same
brand system — pointed outward at the country we get to call home.*

---

## 0. How to read this document

This is the founding brand brief for **EVOLVE Lifestyle**. It is written to be
sorted, argued with, and edited. Everything here is a starting position, not a
locked decision — the parts that are already locked (name, logo, core palette)
are called out explicitly. The website framework in this repo is the first
visual expression of everything below.

## 1. Brand essence & positioning

### One line
**EVOLVE makes Western-Canadian outdoor lifestyle apparel for the people who
built this country in its hardest seasons — and never came inside.**

### The essence (the single feeling)
> *Rugged pride, quietly worn.*

Not loud. Not costume-y. Not "outdoorsy" in the soft, suburban-catalogue sense.
EVOLVE is the feeling of pulling on a heavyweight hoodie before a cold drive to
somewhere with no signal, and knowing exactly who you are. It is heritage you
can wear without explaining.

### Positioning statement
For **proud, capable Canadians who live their lives outside** — trades workers,
hunters, anglers, hikers, snowboarders, and everyone who treats the weekend as
something to be *earned* — **EVOLVE** is an outdoor lifestyle apparel brand that
**turns where we come from into something you can wear.** Unlike the polished,
globalised outdoor giants (or the ironic streetwear that borrows the aesthetic
without the blood), EVOLVE is **the real thing: born in the Alberta trades,
weather-tested, and unapologetically Canadian.**

### Where it sits
- **More rugged than** Aritzia/Roots/lululemon (lifestyle, but soft, urban).
- **More heritage & soul than** Carhartt/Dickies (workwear, but generic, American).
- **More accessible & warmer than** Arc'teryx/Filson (technical or premium-aloof).
- **More authentic than** fashion brands cosplaying the outdoors.

EVOLVE's whitespace: **the proud, blue-collar, big-country middle** — premium
feel, working-class soul, distinctly Western-Canadian.

### The "Revenant" reference (mood, not theme)
The cinematic reference is *The Revenant*: brutal beauty, breath in cold air,
survival as dignity. We borrow the **mood** — the drama, the cold light, the
reverence for the land — never the gore. EVOLVE is dramatic because our customer
base lives dramatically: real weather, real distance, real stakes.

## 2. The three brand stories (expanded)

These are the narrative pillars. They appear on the site (`/about`,
`BrandPillars`), on hang-tags, in campaign copy, and as the spine of every
seasonal drop.

### Story 01 — Descended from Survivors
**The hardest people built the coldest country.**

Before heated cabs and paved highways, people crossed this land on foot,
wintered where the thermometer forgets mercy, and got up the next morning to do
it again. First Peoples who knew the land for millennia; settlers, trappers,
railway crews, and farmers who broke ground in a climate that breaks most
things. That endurance is the inheritance. EVOLVE is a quiet salute to it — to
**grit that doesn't brag, and pride earned in weather that sends most people
indoors.** We're not nostalgic for the hardship; we're proud of the people who
out-lasted it, because their stubbornness is why we're here.

*Use it for:* heritage drops, the heavyweight anchor pieces, Remembrance-season
storytelling, "founder's story" content.

### Story 02 — One of the Largest Lands on Earth
**This is the backyard. All of it.**

Canada is the second-largest country on the planet and most of it is still
wild. Prairies that run flat to the curve of the earth; mountains that don't
care about your schedule; boreal forest; a coast that takes the weather first.
The wildlife was here before us — **bears, caribou, elk, trout** — and *getting
outside isn't a hobby here, it's an identity.* Some things should never change,
and the wild is the big one. EVOLVE exists to send people back into it and to
stand for keeping it the way it found us.

*Use it for:* the four-collection map (Prairies / Peaks / Boreal / Coast),
wildlife graphics, conservation tie-ins, landscape-led campaigns.

### Story 03 — Work Hard, Play Hard
**Strip it back. Build it stronger.**

EVOLVE didn't start in fashion — it started in the trades, **abrasive blasting
and substrate restoration**, stripping surfaces back to bare and rebuilding them
stronger than new. The lifestyle brand is that exact instinct pointed outward:
blue-collar grit plus big-country freedom. **Polite until the work gets hard.**
You put the week in, you wore yourself down, and the outdoors is how you restore
it. *Restore what the week wore down* — then go earn the weekend.

*Use it for:* the link back to the service company, work-to-weekend campaigns,
durability/quality messaging, the "made to be used" product voice.

## 4. The customer

### Primary — "The Capable Canadian" (28–45)
Trades, resource, agriculture, or skilled-services background, or office work
with an outdoor heart. Owns a truck or wants one. Hunts, fishes, sleds, hikes,
or camps. Values gear that's *honest* — does the job, lasts, doesn't pretend.
Proud to be Canadian without being political about it. Buys quality over
quantity, but is price-aware. **Wants to look like himself, only sharper.**

### Secondary — "The Weekend Escapee" (24–38)
Urban or suburban, desk job, lives for the weekend and the off-grid trip.
Aspires to the capability the primary already has. More fashion-aware, more
social-driven, responds to #04, #09, #19. Entry products: caps, tees, beanies.

### Tertiary — "The Proud Gifter" (all ages)
Buying for the hunter/angler/tradesperson in their life. Needs obvious "this is
*so* them" signalling — wildlife graphics, heritage lines, gift-ready packaging.

### What they all share
A belief that **getting outside is identity, not recreation**, and that **where
you're from is worth wearing.**

## 5. Tone of voice

**Polite. Until the work gets hard.** Then it gets direct.

| We are | We are not |
|--------|-----------|
| Proud, grounded, plain-spoken | Boastful, ironic, try-hard |
| Warm but tough | Cold or aloof (we're not Arc'teryx) |
| Canadian-understated | Flag-waving or jingoistic |
| Reverent about the land | Preachy or guilt-trippy |
| Confident, short sentences | Adjective-stuffed marketing-speak |

### Rules (inherited from the EVOLVE house style)
- **Uppercase, tracked headings.** Tight, mechanical, confident.
- **No exclamation points.** Ever. The confidence is in the calm.
- **Oxford comma.** Always.
- Say **abrasive blasting**, not sandblasting; **substrate profiling/restoration**,
  not cleaning (when referencing the service roots).
- Short declaratives. Periods do the work. "Earned outside." not "Get the gear
  you've earned from the great outdoors!"
- Canadian spelling (colour, fibre, neighbour) in body copy.

### Voice in three lengths
- **Stamp:** `EARNED OUTSIDE.`
- **Headline:** `The world goes soft. We go outside.`
- **Paragraph:** "This is apparel for people who measure a good life in cold
  mornings, long drives, and country with no signal. Polite until the work gets
  hard. Proud of where we came from, hungry for where we're headed."

## 6. Visual identity

### 6.1 Logo
- **Same EVOLVE wordmark and system as the service company** — this is one brand
  with two arms. The lifestyle arm uses the wordmark more dramatically (bigger,
  more negative space, more cinematic context).
- **Variants:** metallic/chrome (hero, dark contexts), Alloy Silver (default UI),
  pure white (over busy imagery), Aurora-Neon (accent/stamp only — used sparingly).
- **Real asset source:** Drive — *"Evolve Logos & Media Kit"*
  (`1n77zRHbxLkIL03oXLTg3lWge92m8mU9z`). In this framework the logo is a clean
  SVG wordmark placeholder (`src/components/ui/EvolveLogo.tsx`) — drop the real
  chrome/white files into `/public/brand/` and swap to an `<img>`.
- **Clearspace:** minimum one cap-height of clear space on all sides.
- **Don't:** recolour outside the approved variants, add effects/shadows, stretch,
  or place the neon variant over busy imagery.

### 6.2 Colour
The locked EVOLVE system, used more dramatically:

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Boreal Void** | `#050505` | Base. Almost everything sits on this. |
| Void 900 / 800 / 700 | `#0a0a0a` `#101110` `#16181a` | Elevated surfaces, cards, borders |
| **Aurora Neon** | `#00ff41` | Primary accent — CTAs, highlights, the aurora |
| Aurora Soft | `#4ade80` | Labels, eyebrows, secondary accents |
| Cyber Lime | `#39ff14` | Underlines, glow highlights (use sparingly) |
| **Alloy Silver** | `#c9ced4` | Primary text on dark |
| Silver Bright | `#f3f4f6` | Headlines, emphasis |
| Silver Dim | `#8a9099` | Body / secondary text |

**Discipline:** the palette is 95% Boreal Void + silver, with neon as a *scalpel*,
not a highlighter. The drama comes from contrast and scale, not from colour
saturation. Earth tones (rust, olive, sand, plaid) live **in the product
photography**, not in the UI chrome.

### 6.3 Typography
- **Typeface: Neue Montreal** (Pangram Pangram). The real font is required — **do
  not substitute.** Source: Drive brand assets *"Neue Montreal.zip"*. Drop files
  into `/public/fonts/` (see `public/fonts/README.md`). The framework ships a
  graceful grotesque fallback so it runs before the licensed files are added.
- **Scale: dramatic.** Display type runs to `clamp(4.5rem … 16rem)`. Headlines
  are uppercase, tightly tracked (`-0.03em` to `-0.05em`), line-height < 1.
- **Body:** sentence case, generous line-height (1.5–1.7), Silver Dim.
- **Eyebrows / labels / data:** mono, uppercase, wide tracking (`0.18em–0.28em`),
  Aurora Soft. (Framework uses a mono fallback stack; can be swapped for a
  branded mono later.)
- **Hierarchy is built on scale and weight, not many fonts** — one typeface,
  used with confidence.

### 6.4 Imagery direction
- **Cinematic, cold-light, high-drama landscape & wildlife.** Prairies, peaks,
  boreal, coast. Bears, caribou, elk, trout. Breath in cold air, low sun, fog,
  snow, water.
- **People:** real, capable, unposed. Working, hiking, casting, glassing a
  ridge. Never fashion-vacant or studio-sterile. Faces optional; hands, gear,
  and breath tell the story.
- **Product:** garment-forward, textured, shot like it's already been used.
  Heavy fabric, real grain.
- **Treatment:** deep shadows, retained blacks, slight desaturation toward
  teal-green, a hint of grain. Everything bleeds into Boreal Void at the edges.
- **In this framework:** royalty-free outdoor stock (Unsplash) stands in as
  clearly-swappable placeholder, with an on-brand gradient fallback so the grid
  never breaks. **Replace with owned/commissioned photography before launch.**

### 6.5 Motion (the "expensive" layer)
- **Smooth, weighted, never bouncy.** Easing is expo-out / custom cubic-bezier
  `(0.65, 0.05, 0, 1)`. Things *settle* like heavy fabric.
- Smooth scroll (Lenis), scroll-scrubbed manifesto, word-by-word reveals,
  parallax imagery, magnetic buttons, a cinematic 3D hero (aurora + boreal
  terrain + drifting particles), curtain page transitions, a counting preloader.
- **Restraint is the brand.** Motion serves the content; nothing spins for the
  sake of spinning. All of it respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 7. Product lines

Built drop-ship-first (Printful / Printify), so the line plan favours blanks
that print/embroider beautifully and ship reliably across Canada & North America.

### Core categories
1. **Outerwear (the anchor)** — heavyweight hoodies (480 GSM), brushed flannels
   & overshirts, packable shells, eventually insulated/canvas pieces. *Highest
   price, highest pride, the heart of the brand.*
2. **Tops** — heavyweight tees (240–260 GSM), pocket tees, waffle thermals/base
   layers, long-sleeves, crews.
3. **Headwear** — structured trucker caps, cuff beanies, 5-panels. *The entry
   product and the walking billboard.*
4. **Accessories** — patches, enamel pins, tote/duffel, socks, drinkware,
   bandanas, dog gear. *Gifting and margin.*

### The collection map (ties to Story 02)
Four seasonal/thematic collections mapped to the land:
- **Prairies** — wheat/gold tones, flatland, heritage graphics.
- **Peaks** — alpine, snow, base-layer & technical-leaning.
- **Boreal** — deep green, the heavyweight anchor pieces, wildlife.
- **Coast** — storm/teal, weatherproof, "where the signal dies."

### Graphic/print territories
- Wordmark & wear-it-quiet tonal embroidery (premium tier).
- Wildlife crests (caribou, bear, elk, trout) — line-art, badge style.
- Slogan type (from §3) — back-prints and chest stamps.
- Map/coordinate motifs, topographic lines, "Est." heritage marks.

### Pricing posture (placeholder, CAD)
Caps ~$36–42 · Tees ~$54–58 · Thermals ~$72 · Hoodies ~$128 · Flannels ~$146 ·
Shells ~$189. **Premium-accessible:** priced like you respect the customer's
money *and* the quality. (Mirrors the service company's "fair but never cheap"
posture.)

## 8. Naming & sub-brand notes

- **Master brand:** **EVOLVE.** The lifestyle arm can be referred to as
  **EVOLVE** alone, or **EVOLVE Lifestyle / EVOLVE Outdoors** where disambiguation
  from the blasting company is needed (e.g., legal, invoicing, SEO).
- **Endorsement model:** the service company is the root. Footer and About always
  point back to **Evolve Eco Blasting** ("Strip it back. Build it stronger." is
  the literal bridge line). One logo, two arms — never a separate logo.
- **Collection naming:** land-based (Prairies, Peaks, Boreal, Coast) — see §7.
- **Drop naming:** seasonal + theme, e.g. *"The First Cold Snap,"* *"Long Haul,"*
  *"Trapline."* Keep them plain, evocative, Canadian.
- **Product naming:** function + heritage word — *Survivors Heavyweight Hoodie,
  Boreal Flannel, Caribou Trucker, Long Haul Thermal, Coast Shell, Trapline Pocket
  Tee, Weather-Tested Beanie.* (All used in the framework catalogue.)
- **Domain/social (to confirm):** suggest `evolveoutdoors.ca` / `wearevolve.ca` /
  `evolve.supply` style handles; keep `@evolve...` consistent. **Action item, not
  decided.**
- **The "kit" language:** the cart is "your kit," products are "the range." It
  frames apparel as equipment you assemble, not fashion you consume.
